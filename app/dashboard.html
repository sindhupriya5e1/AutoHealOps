The watchdog can restart the Docker container on the machine where it runs. The public Render demo cannot restart your laptop. No cloud recovery controller is configured yet.

Deployment scope: Public deployment: FastAPI metrics are scraped by the hosted Prometheus service and visualized in hosted Grafana. The PowerShell watchdog and Docker container-recovery demonstration run locally on the project host; they do not restart the public Render service. Public metrics describe the Render container, not your laptop.
